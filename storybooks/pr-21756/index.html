Summary
docs(adr): freeze the passkey wrap format at v1
Description
Because:

- The passkey wrap envelope is a compatibility contract the server
  cannot migrate; a format change after production writes sends every
  passkey user back to their password.
- The policy and its reasoning were spread across five tickets.
- The format became one AES-GCM layer in #21823, and the ADR has to
  record that decision rather than the two-layer design it replaced.

This commit:

- Adds ADR 0053: the freeze, four options, what counts as a format
  change, what holds it in place, and the shape of a v2.
- Records the move from HPKE to one layer and why, with the freeze dated
  from that fixture regeneration.
- Moves the per-value design rationale into the passkey-crypto README.
- Corrects doc comments in the module.
- Adds CODEOWNERS entries for passkey-crypto, libs/accounts/passkey and
  the passkeyWraps migrations.

Closes: FXA-14155
