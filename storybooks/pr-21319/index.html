Summary
chore(payments-next): upgrade to Next.js 16 with Turbopack
Description
Because:

* Next.js 16 makes Turbopack the default bundler and renames
  middleware to proxy.
* @next/bundle-analyzer and two Sentry build options only work with
  webpack.

This commit:

* Bumps next to 16.3.6 and builds payments-next with Turbopack.
* Renames middleware.ts to proxy.ts and its export to proxy.
* Replaces @next/bundle-analyzer with an nx `analyze` target, with a
  NEXT_DIST_DIR override for its distDir bug.
* Removes reactComponentAnnotation and disableLogger from the Sentry
  config.
* Removes the unused `export` target.

Closes PAY-3783, PAY-3784
