## Because

- `showReactApp.signInRoutes` is true in stage and in production. React serves the signin routes, so the Backbone views behind them are unreachable.

- Deletes the Backbone signin views, mixins, models, templates and specs, and their `require` lines in `app/tests/test_start.js`.
- Removes 7 route entries from `app/scripts/lib/router.js`, and converts 7 more to React-only handlers.
- Removes the `showReactApp.signInRoutes` config knob from `server/lib/configuration.js`, `server/config/local.json-dist`, `server/lib/beta-settings.js`, `server/lib/routes/react-app/types.ts` and `packages/fxa-settings/src/lib/config.ts`.
