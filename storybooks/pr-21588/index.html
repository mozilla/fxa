Summary
feat(auth): attribute Monitor web and VPN app sign-ins to their service
Description
Because:
- Monitor web and the Mozilla VPN app sign in without their service's
  canonical scope, so no consent row exists for the Firefox token exchange.
- The accountAuthorizations backfill could not recognise those tokens and
  had no way to resume after a killed pod.
- A disconnect of any allowlisted client deauthorized the fallback row
  while the app was still signed in.

This commit:
- Adds oauthServer.exchange.serviceForClient (client_id -> service), read by
  /oauth/authorization as a last-resort fallback and by the backfill script.
- Writes the service's canonical scope row when the token lacks it, in both
  the sign-in path and the backfill.
- Adds --omit-scope and --start-cursor to the backfill and logs the cursor
  per batch.
- Treats a mapped client's tokens as covering its service's canonical scope
  in authorizationRowsToDeauthorize.
- Ignores mappings for services absent from serviceScopes.

Removal tracked in FXA-14813.
